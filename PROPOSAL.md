# Scholarship Hunter AI

**Narxoz University | Final Project Proposal**

Submission: one per group. **Deadline:** November 11, 2026, 23:59.

**1. Problem and Target Users.** Students from Kazakhstan seeking fully funded master's scholarships abroad must compare scattered announcements and complex eligibility requirements. Scholarship Hunter AI will reduce this effort by turning a student's academic background, language qualifications, and preferences into an explained shortlist of suitable opportunities.

**2. AI Feature and Evaluation.** A local Qwen model will coordinate scholarship discovery, eligibility checking, personalized ranking, gap analysis, and explanations grounded in official sources. Explicit eligibility rules will support the model; missing evidence will produce an "unknown" result. Evaluation will use 20 synthetic student profiles, manually labeled eligibility cases, annotated source fields, and human relevance judgments. Success targets are **Eligibility Accuracy ≥90%** (correct eligibility classifications), **Extraction F1 ≥85%** (precision/recall over funding, deadline, and eligibility fields), **Precision@3 ≥80%** (relevant results among the top three for profiles with at least three relevant opportunities), and **citation coverage 100%** (factual scholarship claims accompanied by supporting official links). Citation support will be manually checked. These are targets, not achieved results.

**3. Agentic Tools and Cost.** The agent will use four tools: `search_scholarships()` to retrieve catalogue candidates; `fetch_official_page()` to retrieve evidence from approved official domains; `check_eligibility()` to compare profile attributes with documented requirements; and `rank_scholarships()` to order candidates using transparent eligibility and preference criteria. The workflow will retrieve, verify, check, and rank before returning a shortlist or a justified no-match response. All tools will be free, with local Ollama/Qwen inference and no paid APIs; existing computer hardware will be used.

**4. MVP and Stretch Goals.** The MVP will provide a student profile form, a curated catalogue of **20–30 scholarships**, eligibility checking, ranked recommendations, gap explanations, and official source links. Funding coverage will be verified before an opportunity is labeled fully funded. Two optional stretch goals are **deadline tracking** and a **personalized application roadmap**.

**5. Data and APIs.** Data will come from official [Erasmus Mundus](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/opportunities/individuals/students/erasmus-mundus-joint-masters) and [DAAD](https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/scholarships/daad-scholarships/) scholarship pages, with manual review, deduplication, source URLs, and verification dates. **React and TypeScript** will provide the interface; **FastAPI and Python** will expose the application API and coordinate tools; **SQLite** will store scholarship records and minimal profiles; **Ollama** will serve **Qwen** locally through its API. Official web pages will supply evidence without requiring a third-party scholarship API. Stale information will be flagged, source content treated as untrusted data, and personal information kept local.
